Hadith: 1
Book: Revelation
Narrator: Umar ibn al-Khattab
Theme: Intention, sincerity
Summary: Deeds are judged by the intentions behind them, and each person receives what he intended. Whoever migrates for the sake of God and His Messenger, his migration counts as such; whoever migrates for worldly gain or to marry a woman, his migration counts only for what he sought.

Hadith: 2
Book: Belief
Narrator: Abdullah ibn Umar
Theme: Pillars of Islam
Summary: Islam is built upon five foundations: testifying that there is no god but God and that Muhammad is His Messenger, establishing prayer, giving obligatory charity, performing pilgrimage to the House, and fasting the month of Ramadan.

Hadith: 3
Book: Belief
Narrator: Anas ibn Malik
Theme: Brotherhood, love for others
Summary: None of you truly believes until he wishes for his brother what he wishes for himself.

Hadith: 4
Book: Belief
Narrator: Abdullah ibn Amr
Theme: Restraint of tongue and hand
Summary: A Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand other Muslims are safe, and a migrant is one who abandons what God has forbidden.

Hadith: 5
Book: Knowledge
Narrator: Muawiyah
Theme: Seeking knowledge
Summary: When God intends good for a person, He grants him understanding of religion.

Hadith: 6
Book: Knowledge
Narrator: Abdullah ibn Amr
Theme: Conveying knowledge
Summary: Convey from me even a single verse. Knowledge is not seized from people directly, but through the passing away of the learned, until the ignorant are taken as guides and lead others astray.

Hadith: 7
Book: Ablution
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Purity, prayer
Summary: The prayer of a person who breaks his state of purity is not accepted until he performs ablution again.

Hadith: 8
Book: Prayer Times
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Regularity of prayer
Summary: If a river ran at the door of one of you and he bathed in it five times a day, no trace of dirt would remain on him. Such is the likeness of the five daily prayers, by which God erases sins.

Hadith: 9
Book: Friday Prayer
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Friday, cleanliness
Summary: Washing on Friday is due upon every adult, along with cleaning the teeth and using perfume if available.

Hadith: 10
Book: Obligatory Charity
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Charity, generosity
Summary: Charity does not decrease wealth. God increases the honour of one who forgives, and whoever humbles himself for God's sake is raised in rank by Him.

Hadith: 11
Book: Fasting
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Ramadan, forgiveness
Summary: Whoever fasts Ramadan out of faith and hope of reward will have his previous sins forgiven.

Hadith: 12
Book: Fasting
Narrator: Sahl ibn Sad
Theme: Reward of fasting
Summary: There is a gate in Paradise called Ar-Rayyan through which only those who fasted will enter on the Day of Resurrection. When the last of them has entered, it is closed.

Hadith: 13
Book: Pilgrimage
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Hajj, purification
Summary: Whoever performs the pilgrimage without obscenity or wrongdoing returns free of sin, as on the day his mother bore him.

Hadith: 14
Book: Sales and Trade
Narrator: Hakim ibn Hizam
Theme: Honesty in trade
Summary: Both parties in a transaction may reconsider before they part. If they are truthful and disclose defects, their transaction is blessed; if they conceal and lie, the blessing of their transaction is erased.

Hadith: 15
Book: Manners
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Speech, hospitality
Summary: Whoever believes in God and the Last Day should speak well or remain silent, should be generous to his neighbour, and should be generous to his guest.

Hadith: 16
Book: Manners
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Anger, self-control
Summary: The strong person is not the one who overcomes others in wrestling, but the one who controls himself when angry.

Hadith: 17
Book: Manners
Narrator: Abdullah ibn Amr
Theme: Kindness to parents
Summary: God's pleasure lies in the pleasure of the parent, and God's displeasure lies in the displeasure of the parent.

Hadith: 18
Book: Medicine
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Healing, illness
Summary: God has sent down no illness without also sending down a cure for it.

Hadith: 19
Book: Oppression
Narrator: Anas ibn Malik
Theme: Justice, helping others
Summary: Help your brother whether he is the oppressor or the oppressed. When asked how one helps an oppressor, the answer was: by restraining him from oppression.

Hadith: 20
Book: Softening of Hearts
Narrator: Ibn Abbas
Theme: Health, free time
Summary: Two blessings that many people fail to make use of are good health and free time.

Hadith: 21
Book: Softening of Hearts
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Mercy of God
Summary: God divided mercy into one hundred parts, keeping ninety-nine with Himself and sending one part to earth, from which all creatures show compassion to one another, so that even an animal lifts its hoof away from its young.

Hadith: 22
Book: Good Manners
Narrator: Aisha
Theme: Gentleness
Summary: Gentleness beautifies whatever it accompanies, and its absence leaves whatever it is removed from disfigured.

Hadith: 23
Book: Companions
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Speech and its consequences
Summary: A person may utter a word pleasing to God without weighing it, and be raised in rank; and may utter a word displeasing to God without weighing it, and fall far because of it.

Hadith: 24
Book: Marriage
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Choosing a spouse
Summary: A woman is married for four things: her wealth, her lineage, her beauty, and her religion. Choose the one with religion and you will prosper.

Hadith: 25
Book: Oaths and Vows
Narrator: Abu Hurayrah
Theme: Truthfulness
Summary: Signs of hypocrisy are three: when he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, and when he is trusted he betrays the trust.


Story: 1
Title: UBUNTU — "Ben, biz olduğumuz zaman benim"
Source: Yaşanmış küçük öyküler, No. 8
Language: Türkçe
Theme: Dayanışma, paylaşmak, birlik olmak
Text: Bir zaman bir bilim adamı Afrika'da görev yapıyormuş. Oranın yerli çocuklarına oyun oynamayı teklif etmiş. Bir ağacın altına meyveler koymuş ve ağaca ilk ulaşan kişinin ödül olarak bütün meyveleri yiyebileceğini söylemiş. Çocukları sıraya dizmiş ve yarışı başlatmış. Fakat bilim adamının beklemediği bir olay olmuş: çocuklar el ele tutuşmuş ve beraber koşmaya başlamışlar. Adam bu duruma çok şaşırmış ve çocuklara neden böyle yaptıklarını sormuş. Çocuklar "Bu UBUNTU'dur" demişler. "Diğerleri mutsuz iken bir kişi nasıl meyveleri yiyebilir ki? Biz ubuntu yaparak hepimiz yedik. Ubuntu bizim dilimizde 'ben, biz olduğumuz zaman benim' demektir."

Story: 2
Title: Ubuntu kavramı
Source: Yaşanmış küçük öyküler, No. 8
Language: Türkçe
Theme: Ubuntu felsefesi, Afrika kültürü
Text: Ubuntu, Afrika kültüründe insanların birbirlerine bağlılık ve ilişkilerine odaklanan ahlaki ve insani bir anlayıştır. Sözcük Güney Afrika'daki Bantu dillerinden gelmektedir. Temel fikri şudur: bir insanın varlığı ve iyiliği, topluluğun varlığı ve iyiliğinden ayrılamaz.

Story: 3
Title: Paylaşmak üzerine
Source: Yaşanmış küçük öyküler, No. 8
Language: Türkçe
Theme: Komşuluk, paylaşmak, ekmek
Text: Bizde ise "komşusu açken tok yatan bizden değildir" anlayışı vardır. Büyüklerimiz ne güzel söylemişler: "Eğer tadını bilirseniz, ekmeği paylaşmak ekmekten daha lezzetlidir." Tüm güzelliklerimizi paylaşmak ve güzel bir hayat yaşamak dileğiyle.
